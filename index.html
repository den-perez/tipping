Coffee

The Tipping Point Coffee is a spot for the homies, the creatives and the people. A place where culture, community and good energy meet.

“From food and sneakers to hats and music, David blended all the things that make his world feel like home and brought that energy into a coffee shop.”

@coolxdad, CoolxGuide

“Whisked matcha and espresso tonics… always.”

Food

Before the coffee bar there was the kitchen. Twenty years of pop-ups, plates and a culinary career that earned the name “the hood Anthony Bourdain.” These days the coffee bar cooks too: breakfast, weekend specials and pop-up plates.

“From opening the first shop of its kind in the city, to producing crazy pop ups with streetwear and hip hop legends, to even my culinary career.”

David, @david__tipping

Kicks

The store that ignited streetwear in Houston, back in 2006. Two decades later the sneakers have an adidas tag on them.

“Sneaker game is coming back right.”

At the Unheard Of “Oink Oink” launch, boxes in hand and every colorway on the floor. Plus a Lone Star style session in the adidas Superstar II.

Hats

David has been cooking up fitteds for years: Astros reworks, beer packs, tributes to Dipset and Chalino Sánchez, and a cap inspired by his own Mercedes.

“Hoy Se Bebe.”

A five-pack of New Era 59FIFTYs for Hat Club, packed with Mexican flair and dropped to open Hispanic Heritage Month.

Rides

The GX550 Overtrail

Built out with Adventure Fitter Solutions in Katy: Dobinson suspension and steel bumper, a Westcott collar lift, 35-inch Nitto tires and a rooftop tent. Then taken where it was built to go, from the Piney Woods to Marfa.

“I bought this car to have fun, not to have a pavement princess.”

David, @david__tipping

The Merc

A 1993 Mercedes-Benz 300CE coupe, built in the spirit of the 90s “dope boyz” cars. Field green up top, moss green below. It’s well known enough that it got its own fitted.
